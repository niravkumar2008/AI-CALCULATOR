# Prototype photo shot list

For when the first working calculator is assembled. A phone camera is enough.
Real photos of the real device are the most convincing thing the website can show.

## Setup (10 minutes, no special gear)

- **Light:** stand next to a big window on a cloudy day, or with the sun not shining straight in. No overhead room lights, no flash. Turn off any lamps (mixed light colours look muddy).
- **Background:** a big sheet of plain white or light grey poster board, curved up a wall so there's no visible corner. For "in use" shots, a clean desk.
- **Bounce:** a second white sheet (or printer paper) on the side away from the window fills in the dark side.
- **Phone:** wipe the lens. Use the main 1× camera (or 2× for less distortion), not the wide one. Tap to focus on the screen, then drag exposure down a little so the white background isn't blown out. Shoot at full resolution, no filters, no portrait-mode blur.
- **Calculator:** clean fingerprints off the shell and the window with a microfibre cloth. Have a real problem on the screen (the demo: `3/4 + 2^5 × sin 30°`).

## The shots (in priority order)

| # | Shot | How | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero, front ¾** | Calculator at a slight angle on the white board, screen showing an AI Solve answer. Phone a bit above, angled down ~30°. | Hero image |
| 2 | **In hand, aiming at homework** | Hold it over a printed or handwritten problem, back camera facing the paper, viewfinder on screen. Shoot from over the shoulder. | "How it works" step 1 |
| 3 | **Screen close-up: steps** | Straight on, close, the steps screen filling most of the frame. Check there's no window reflection on the glass. | "See every step" |
| 4 | **Back, camera window** | Flip it over, ¾ angle, so the camera window is clear. | Specs / features |
| 5 | **Magnetic charging** | The magnetic cable just clicking on, plug in sharp focus. | Features |
| 6 | **On a desk, in context** | With a notebook, pencil and textbook, from above (flat-lay), calculator at a slight angle. | Section backgrounds |
| 7 | **Thin side profile** | Low, side-on, on the white board. | Specs |
| 8 | **Inside (optional)** | Back cover off, the green board visible, good light. | "Built for real" / trust |
| 9 | **Short video, 5–10 s** | Hand presses `MODE 4 =`, the answer appears. Phone on a stack of books, landscape. | Hero or "How it works" |

Take 5–10 tries of each, moving slightly between them. Pick the best later.

## Don'ts

- No Casio logo visible anywhere (cover it, or use the unbranded shell). The site says "Not affiliated with Casio".
- No exam rooms, test papers, or anything that looks like it's being used in an exam.
- No faces of anyone under 18 unless a parent agrees in writing. Hands only is safest.
- Don't edit the screen contents in an app: what's on screen must be what the calculator really showed.

## After shooting

Put the originals in `website/assets/photos/originals/`. Then ask Claude to pick the best, straighten, crop to 16:9 / 4:5 / 1:1, colour-match them, and export web sizes (AVIF/WebP + JPEG fallback).
