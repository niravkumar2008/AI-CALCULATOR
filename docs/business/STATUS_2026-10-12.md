## How to try it

- **Website and server, on your own computer** (fake payments, fake AI, free): in the launch-site worktree run
  `node web/dev/run-local.mjs`, then open **http://127.0.0.1:8787**. Sign-in emails land in a local outbox.
  `node web/dev/calculator.mjs new` pretends to be a calculator so you can link one. `--fresh` wipes the data.
- **Calculator**: double-click **`Proofline_Simulator_v15.exe`** in the root of the v15 worktree. Press MODE
  to see all the modes; MODE 4 opens Apps.
- The site's `/try-it/` page has the same calculator running in the browser.
