## How to use this

- **One video, three places.** Every "Post" goes to TikTok, then the clean file (no watermark) to
  YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels the same day. "IG carousel" is Instagram only.
- **Cadence:** weeks 1 to 4: 4 posts a week (Mon, Wed, Fri + Sunday build update). Weeks 5 to 12:
  5 posts a week (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat + Sunday build update). That sits in the 2-to-5-a-week sweet
  spot from Buffer's study [S39 in MARKET_RESEARCH.md].
- **Post time:** after school and evening, US Eastern, about 4 to 8 pm on weekdays, late morning on
  weekends (my estimate; check your own analytics after week 3 and adjust).
- **Milestone gates.** Some posts need a real event. Dates for these are targets, not promises.
  If the milestone hasn't happened, **post a backup from the bank (B1 to B12) instead** and move
  the milestone post to the day it really happens. Never fake a milestone.

| Gate | Milestone | Target (not a promise) |
|---|---|---|
| **M1** | Site deployed on the real domain, waitlist form tested | Week 1 to 2 |
| **M2** | v15 boards arrive | Weeks 2 to 4 |
| **M3** | First power-on of sample 1 | Weeks 3 to 5 |
| **M4** | Sample 1 assembled in the shell, keys and screen working | Weeks 4 to 6 |
| **M5** | AI Solve works on the real device (photo to steps) | Weeks 5 to 8 |
| **M6** | Exam mode works on the real device | Weeks 5 to 8 |
| **M7** | All 5 samples built | Weeks 8 to 12 |

**Before M1, don't put a link anywhere.** Posts in week 1 can say "waitlist opening soon";
switch the bio link on the day the form works.

**Every post:** run the compliance checklist (MARKETING_GUIDE section 13). Casio logo never
visible. Renders labelled "CAD render". No faces of minors. No banned words.
