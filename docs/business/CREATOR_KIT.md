## Part A. The brief (send this to the creator)

### A1. Who we work with

- **18 or older. No exceptions.** TikTok's branded-content tools and Meta's creator marketplace are
  18+ [S45]. We check ID or platform verification before anything ships.
- STEM and study creators, college students, math and science teachers and tutors, gadget and
  maker reviewers. An audience that is mostly 18+ (we'll ask for your audience age stats).

### A2. What Proofline is (the facts you can use)

**One line:** Proofline is a real scientific calculator with an AI tutor that shows every step, and
an exam mode a teacher can see.

- **AI Solve:** MODE 4 → AI Solve. The screen becomes a live camera viewfinder. Press = to take a
  photo of a problem; the answer and every step appear on the calculator. Math and science, plus
  basic finance. If the handwriting is unclear, it says so instead of guessing. Needs Wi-Fi.
- **Type it:** type a problem on the keys instead of taking a photo.
- **A full scientific calculator, offline:** fractions, trig, logs, memory, replay; STAT (MODE 2)
  with 7 regression types; TABLE (MODE 3); CALC and SOLVE; definite integrals and derivatives.
  Standard US key layout, real rubber keymat. Classic LCD look on the calculator screens; color in
  the apps.
- **Apps (MODE 4):** AI Solve, Type it, History, Notes, Geography, Periodic Table, Settings. All but
  AI Solve and Type it work offline.
- **Exam mode:** SHIFT MODE 8 =. The AI, camera, Wi-Fi and every app turn off. The status bar turns
  black with a padlock, "EXAM" and a timer. The calculator, STAT, TABLE, CALC, SOLVE, ∫dx and d/dx
  keep working. Ends after 12 hours, or a teacher unlocks it with a USB cable.
- **Not allowed on the SAT, ACT or AP exams, even in exam mode** (it has Wi-Fi and a camera).
- **Price:** $225 for the calculator. The AI tutor plan is $10 a month or $100 a year. First month
  free, from when you first link the calculator, no card needed. No free tier after that. Cancel
  anytime. Without the plan it's still a full offline scientific calculator.
- **Photos:** used only to solve that problem, not stored.
- **Status:** 5 hand-built samples. Preorders planned for 2027. Not for sale yet. US only.

### A3. Must-say (somewhere in every video)

1. **The disclosure** (section A5), in the first seconds.
2. **"It's not allowed on the SAT, ACT or AP."** (or "not for standardized tests").
3. **The full price**, if you mention price at all: "$225, plus $10 a month or $100 a year for the
   AI tutor plan; first month free."
4. **"AI can make mistakes; that's why it shows every step."** if accuracy comes up.
5. **"It's a prototype sample"** and that it isn't on sale yet ("join the waitlist").

### A4. What to show, and what not to show

**Great to show**

- AI Solve on a problem you wrote by hand: viewfinder, press =, the steps scrolling.
- The honest moments: the "can't read it" message on messy writing, then a retake.
- **Exam mode, positively:** turn it on, show the black bar with padlock, EXAM and timer, step back
  so viewers see it's visible from across a room, press MODE 4 to show "Apps are off". Frame it as
  "this is how a teacher knows the AI is off". Show that STAT, TABLE and SOLVE still work.
- Offline calculator work: fractions, trig, statistics, a table.
- The apps: Periodic Table, Geography quiz, Notes.
- Studying without your phone: desk set-up, phone away.
- Anything you didn't like. Honest opinions are the point.

**Never show or say**

- **The original shell maker's logo.** The samples are built in purchased calculator shells; the
  maker's logo on them must stay covered (the sticker we put on it stays on) or out of frame in
  every shot. Don't mention the shell's brand or model. Proofline is not affiliated with Casio
  Computer Co., Ltd. [Note for Nirav, delete before sending: this means the Casio logo on the
  sample shells; `MARKETING_GUIDE.md` 1.4.]
- **Cheating jokes**, "hide it from the teacher", "they'll never know", "pass any test", using it
  in a real test, or any banned word: cheat/cheating (except "it's not for cheating"),
  undetectable, stealth, hidden or secret mode, panic or hide button, exam-approved.
- Filming in a real exam or a classroom during class. Anyone under 18 on camera. Students' faces.
- Copyrighted textbook pages or worksheets (write your own problems).
- Claims we can't back: "100% accurate", "never wrong", "replaces a tutor", "better grades",
  battery-life numbers, screen size, ship dates, "in stock".
- Names of any AI company or payment company behind the product, or words like "API", "server".
- Comparisons that name other products or apps.
- Trending songs: use the platform's commercial sound library only.

### A5. Disclosure (required by law and by the platforms)

The FTC says the disclosure must be clear and **in the video itself**, not only in the caption or
your bio; a free or loaned product counts as a material connection; "ad", "#ad" and "sponsored"
are fine; "sp", "collab" and "thanks Proofline" alone are not [S46].

Do all three:

1. **Say it** in the first few seconds: "This is an ad. Proofline paid me and loaned me this
   sample." (If unpaid: "Proofline loaned me this sample for free.")
2. **Show it** on screen in the first seconds, big enough to read: **"#ad · paid partnership with
   Proofline · loaned sample"**.
3. **Turn on the platform's label:** TikTok "Paid partnership" / branded-content toggle; Instagram
   "Paid partnership" label; YouTube "Includes paid promotion" box. Also put "#ad" at the start of
   the caption.

Live streams: say it at the start and repeat it every so often. Stories/short clips: disclose on
every clip.

### A6. Talking points (use your own words)

- "It shows every step, so I can see where I went wrong."
- "It says when it can't read my handwriting instead of making something up."
- "Same key layout as the calculator I already use."
- "Exam mode: one key sequence and the AI is off, and anyone can see it."
- "Without the plan it's still a full calculator; nothing stops working offline."
- "I can study without picking up my phone."
- "It's not allowed on the SAT, ACT or AP, and they say that themselves."
- "It's a prototype. They're building 5 samples, then production. Waitlist's in my bio."

### A7. Our review

We check your draft for **facts and the rules above only** (wrong price, banned words, logo
visible, missing disclosure). We never ask you to change your opinion or tone, and we never pay
for positive content [S47].

## Part E. Tracking links

The site reads these from any page link and saves them with the waitlist sign-up (first link of
the visit wins) (`web/site/assets/js/site.js`):

- `utm_source` (who): the creator's tag, e.g. `c-alex`
- `utm_medium` (what kind): `creator`
- `utm_campaign` (which push): e.g. `waitlist-2027q1` or `kickstarter-launch`
- `ref` (referral code, optional): e.g. `alex`
- The short form `?src=c-alex` also counts as `utm_source`.

**Tag rules:** lower-case letters, digits, `-` or `_`, up to 40 characters. Anything else is
silently ignored (`C. Alex` won't work; `c-alex` will).

**Link pattern:**

```
https://proofline.example/?utm_source=c-<name>&utm_medium=creator&utm_campaign=<campaign>
```

Examples:

```
https://proofline.example/?utm_source=c-alex&utm_medium=creator&utm_campaign=waitlist-2027q1
https://proofline.example/try-it/?utm_source=c-mrsk&utm_medium=creator&utm_campaign=teachers-2027q1
https://proofline.example/?src=c-alex          (short version for a bio)
```

For a paid boost of the same video, change the medium so you can tell them apart:
`utm_medium=paid-boost`.

**Counting sign-ups** (admin only, `docs/web/GUIDE.md` 8.1):
`SELECT count(*) FROM waitlist WHERE utm_source = 'c-alex'`.

Kickstarter tip: on campaign day, Kickstarter has its own referral tags for links to the campaign
page; set those up in Kickstarter's dashboard and note them in the same table.
