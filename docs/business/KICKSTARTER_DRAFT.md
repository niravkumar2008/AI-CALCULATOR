### 2.2 Why we built it

Most students already use AI for homework. Most of them also worry it stops them thinking for
themselves. A phone gives you the answer, then a feed, then three notifications.

We wanted the opposite: a calculator you already know how to use, with a tutor inside that shows
every line of the working, so you can follow it and check it. No feed. No ads. No phone on the
desk.

And we wanted it to be honest with teachers. Some "AI calculators" are sold on hiding the AI. Ours
does the opposite: one key sequence switches the AI off, and the screen shows it, in a way anyone
can see from the front of the room.

**A tutor, not a shortcut.**

### 2.3 What it does

**AI Solve.** Press MODE 4 and open AI Solve. The screen becomes a live camera viewfinder. Point the
camera on the back at a problem and press = to take the photo. The answer and every step appear on
the calculator. It works for math and science, plus basic finance. If the handwriting is unclear,
it says so instead of guessing. AI can make mistakes. That's why it shows every step.

**Type it.** No photo? Type the problem on the keys (up to 160 characters) and get the same
step-by-step answer.

**A full scientific calculator, offline.** Standard US key layout and a real rubber keymat.
Fractions, trig, logs, memory and replay. STAT (MODE 2): one- and two-variable statistics with 7
regression types. TABLE (MODE 3): f(x) tables up to 30 rows. CALC and SOLVE. Definite integrals and
derivatives at a point. The calculator screens keep a classic LCD look.

**Apps (MODE 4), in color.** AI Solve, Type it, History (your last 20 answers), Notes (10 short
notes), Geography (177 countries, capitals and a quiz), Periodic Table (all 118 elements) and
Settings. Everything except AI Solve and Type it works offline, with or without the plan.

**Exam mode anyone can see.** SHIFT MODE 8 =. The AI, camera, Wi-Fi and every app turn off (notes
and saved answers too). The calculator, STAT, TABLE, CALC, SOLVE, ∫dx and d/dx keep working. The
status bar turns black with a padlock, the word EXAM and a timer that counts up. Students can't
turn it off from the keys: it ends after 12 hours, or a teacher unlocks it with a USB cable.

**Where you can't use it.** Not allowed on the SAT, ACT or AP exams, even in exam mode, because it
has Wi-Fi and a camera. Bring a standard calculator to those. For a school test, ask your teacher
first: use exam mode, or leave it at home.

**Your photos.** Photos are used only to solve that problem and are not stored. We keep only usage
counts.

## 8. Shipping and limits

- **US addresses only.** Set Kickstarter's shipping to "United States only".
- One shipping charge per calculator, set from a real carrier quote for a small parcel with a
  lithium battery, plus about 10 % for fees.
- Buyers 18+ for calculator rewards.
- No add-ons at launch (keep the pledge manager simple).
- Plan rewards are tied to the backer's account; the 12 months start after the free first month.
