For Nirav. Written 2026-10-11. **Drafts only: nothing has been sent, and no email tool account
exists.** Setting up the email tool and pressing send are yours to do.

Facts only from `web/site-src/PAGE_BRIEF.md`. Voice: short, plain, no exclamation marks, no hype

## 0. Rules for every email

### 0.1 CAN-SPAM (US law) [S55]

- [ ] **A real postal address** in the footer: `Proofline · [street or PO box, city, state, ZIP]`.
      A PO box registered with the Postal Service is fine.
- [ ] **A working unsubscribe link** in every email. Honour it within 10 business days (your email
      tool does this instantly; never re-add someone). The link must keep working for at least 30
      days after the send. Don't ask for anything beyond the email address to unsubscribe.
- [ ] **Honest "From" and "Reply-to":** "Nirav at Proofline" or "Proofline", from your own domain.
- [ ] **Honest subject lines** that match what's inside. No "Re:" or "Fwd:" tricks, no fake
      urgency.
- [ ] **Only email people who signed up.** Never buy, rent or import lists. (People who signed up
      have given consent, so we don't need an "advertisement" label, but keep the content honest.)
- [ ] Footer line: "You're getting this because you joined the Proofline waitlist at
      proofline.example."

### 0.2 Double opt-in (do this before the first send)

The waitlist form today saves the email straight away; **double opt-in is still open**
(`MARKETING_GUIDE.md` 7.2). Recommended: when someone signs up, send one "Confirm your email" email
and only add them to the list after they click. It keeps out typos and fake sign-ups, protects your
sender reputation, and gives you proof of consent. It isn't required by CAN-SPAM, but it's the
standard good practice.

> **Subject:** Confirm your email for the Proofline waitlist
>
> Hi,
>
> Please confirm you want to join the Proofline waitlist:
>
> **[Confirm my email]**
>
> If you didn't sign up, ignore this email and you won't hear from us again.
>
> Proofline · [postal address]

### 0.3 Our own content rules

- No vendor names (AI, payments). No "Casio". No banned words.
- Price always includes the plan: $225 + $10 a month or $100 a year, first month free, no card.
- No ship dates, "in stock", countdowns, "only X left", reviews or user counts.
- "Not allowed on the SAT, ACT or AP" whenever tests come up.
- Real photos only; renders labelled "CAD render".
- Plain-text-friendly: the email must make sense with images off.
