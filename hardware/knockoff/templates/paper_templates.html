Proofline knockoff · paper template 1: back cover (camera spot)

Print at 100 % / "Actual size", then measure the 50 mm bar at the bottom. Cut along the solid outline.

  1. Lay the calculator keys down on the table, top (screen) end away from you. Lay the cut-out on the back, printed side up, matching its outline to the back cover's edges.
  2. Tape it down. Press a pin or the tip of a compass through the red dot to mark the camera centre. Remove the paper.
  3. Drill a 2 mm pilot on the mark, then open it to 7 mm (step drill) from the outside. Deburr.

Paper template 2: top edge (magnet notch)

  1. Cut along the solid outline and cut the yellow window out.
  2. Hold the closed calculator with the keys facing you, then tip the top (screen) end towards you so you look at the top edge. Fold the two end tabs on the dashed lines.
  3. Wrap the strip on the top edge: the end marked RIGHT on the calculator's right-hand side (the solar-strip side), the red seam line on the seam, back-cover side up. Tape it.
  4. Trace the window with a sharp pencil: that's the notch in the front shell. Its top edge sits on the seam; the back cover's lip behind it gets snipped to the same width.

No printer? Measure it instead (0.5 mm pencil, steel ruler, calipers)

Camera: on the back, find the middle between the two long sides and mark a line down the centre. Measure 40.1 mm down from the top edge on that line. That point is the camera centre.

Magnet notch: keys facing you, top edge towards you. From the right-hand outer edge, mark 6.4 mm and 27.9 mm along the top wall. Between those marks the notch goes from the seam towards the keys, 7.95 mm deep.

These positions come from the CAD model built from your photos (good to about ±1.5 mm). Before drilling the first back cover, check the camera mark lands between back-cover ribs 2 and 3 on the inside (35.0 and 45.2 mm below the top edge).
