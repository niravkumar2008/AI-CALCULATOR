Snapshot 2026-10-10

A scientific calculator that photographs a problem and gets it solved by Claude.

A custom board (ESP32-S3, camera, Wi-Fi, magnetic charging) inside a Casio fx-115ES shell. Without a subscription it's still an ordinary scientific calculator; the AI solve needs the $15/month plan. The plan is to market it as a homework and study tool, because the SAT and ACT ban calculators with a camera or Wi-Fi.

$225 + $15/month, 1st month free 500 solves/month cap v14 e-paper: ordered 10/8 v15 LCD: verified ORDER, not ordered

Sources: Claude outputs/LAUNCH_ROADMAP.md §0 (price, SAT/ACT, works without subscription), hardware/QUESTIONS_AND_ISSUES.md (proxy: 30-day trial, 500 cap), hardware/FINAL_STATUS.md "Current plan". Renders: hardware/enclosure/final_assembly/renders/, final_assembly_v15_lcd/renders/.

How it works

Press AI solve: the camera takes a photo, the ESP32-S3 sends it over Wi-Fi to our proxy server, and the proxy (which holds the API key) asks Claude and sends the answer back to the screen.

Sources: hardware/FINAL_STATUS.md §3 and decisions log (TCA8418, RT9080, charge 50 mA, J3 order, contacts never carry battery voltage, 3.6 V gate), hardware/stage15_lcd.md §1, hardware/QUESTIONS_AND_ISSUES.md (proxy: FastAPI, device tokens, TRIAL_DAYS=30, MONTHLY_CAP=500), hardware/production/UNIT_ECONOMICS.md §4.
