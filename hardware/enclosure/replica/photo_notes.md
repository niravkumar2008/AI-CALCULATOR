## Conclusions used in the model
1. Back cover = tray, outline = traced silhouette at 161 x 79.3 (C3). Front shell = the same outline, flush at the top and screen section, inset 0.9 mm per side below the screen (0.8 tray wall = C5, plus 0.1 gap) and 1.1 mm at the bottom end (gives exactly C1 = 159.9). Tray wall height is a guess (no side photo).
2. The 4.7 mm "wall" beside the screen (C4) = 1.2 skin + ~2.5 mm wire channel + 1.0 inner rib, with 3 horizontal pins per side.
3. Key openings: tab shape (small top corners, big bottom corners); SHIFT/ALPHA/MODE/ON ellipses; thin bevel on top; collars + full-width row ribs underneath.
4. Display: bevelled recessed bezel, lens in it. Solar: separate through-window with a cell behind it.
5. Keymat: board-sized rectangle with 2 notches.
6. Back: battery = round hole (with notch) in a shallow rectangular recess, lid modelled as a thin plate (guess); 7th hole in the centre over the small ring; low ribs in the screen section, tall ones in the keypad section; pairs of short ribs on the inside of the side walls.
7. Colours: silver front, navy back, dark grey keys.

   big ring, Y 31 -> 8) to 9.8 and form the side walls round the keypad and the bottom end. The silver part steps in
   0.95 there (navy band visible from the front) and has only a short skirt (to Z 8.3, guess) inside the navy wall.
2. **No photo shows a rail groove** or the slide case itself. The groove in the model is a placeholder guess
   (questions 2-4 in `hardware/QUESTIONS_AND_ISSUES.md`).
