## After it arrives (before anything is plugged in)
1. **Battery:** red wire vs J4 "+". If it's reversed, swap the crimp pins.
2. **Camera ribbon:** fingers 2 and 15 beep to GND.
3. **Magnet piece, before gluing:** with the real cable on a phone charger, find the +5 V leg and put it in **J3 pin 1** (silk "N" / "+"). The GND leg goes in **pin 4** (silk "−"). D−/D+ must beep through to USB-A pins 2/3. Full steps: `ORDER_WALKTHROUGH.md` → "Magnet piece".
4. **First power-up** from USB only. TP5 = 3.3 V, TP7 = EN, TP4 = GND, TP1 = BOOT, TP2/TP3 = UART.
5. **Recovery if flashing ever fails:** hold TP1 → TP4, tap TP7 → GND, flash. Never burn the security or USB-disable eFuses.
