Quick fixes

The things most likely to trip you up, and what to do.

AI Solve won’t connect

AI Solve needs Wi-Fi. Check that the calculator is on 2.4 GHz home Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot, and that exam mode is off. Everything else works offline.

More in Wi-Fi help.

It won’t join the school Wi-Fi

Networks with a login page aren’t supported. Use a phone hotspot or home Wi-Fi.

Exam mode is on and I need AI Solve

That’s expected: exam mode turns off the AI, the camera and Wi-Fi. It ends by itself after 12 hours, or a teacher unlocks it with a USB cable.

The link page won’t take my code

Check you’ve typed all 8 characters exactly as the calculator shows them, and that you’re signed in. If it still fails, show a new code on the calculator and try again, or scan its QR code with your phone.

I can’t add another calculator

An account can have up to 3 calculators. Remove one from your account page to link another. A removed calculator needs linking again to use AI Solve.

The calculator says the free month is over

To keep using AI Solve, subscribe to the AI tutor plan ($10 a month or $100 a year) from your account page. The calculator keeps working offline either way.

It says the problem is unclear

It says so instead of guessing when the handwriting is hard to read. Take the photo again with the problem filling the viewfinder.

Contact us

Still stuck? Email us.

hello@proofline.example

Students, teachers and account holders are all welcome to write.

What to include

  • What you were trying to do, and what happened instead.
  • Any message on the calculator’s screen, word for word.
  • The email address on your account, if you have one.

What not to send

  • Passwords of any kind, including your Wi-Fi password. We’ll never ask for one.
  • The calculator’s link code. We don’t need it to help you.
