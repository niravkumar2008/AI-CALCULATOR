Set up your calculator.

Six steps, from the first charge to your first AI Solve. Wi-Fi takes a minute, and you link it once.

This guide follows the prototype. Exact menu names and key steps may change on the final firmware, and we’ll update this page when they do.

  1. 1

    Charge it

    Snap the magnetic charging connector onto the calculator and let it charge. It has a rechargeable battery.

  2. 2

    Join Wi-Fi

    Press SHIFT then MODE to open SETUP, and choose Wi-Fi.

    SHIFT then MODE

    Then pick one:

    • Type on keys: choose your network and type its password on the calculator’s keypad.
    • Set up with phone: follow the steps on the calculator’s screen to do it from your phone.

    Use 2.4 GHz home Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot. School networks with a login page aren’t supported.

  3. 3

    It registers itself

    The first time it joins Wi-Fi, the calculator registers itself. There are no codes or web addresses to type for this part.

  4. 4

    Link it to your account

    To use AI Solve, the account holder (18 or older) links the calculator to their account once, on any phone or computer:

    • Sign in at the sign-in page.
    • Open Link a calculator.
    • On the calculator, press MODE then 4 to open Apps, then choose Settings and Link calculator to show the code.
    • Enter the 8-character code shown on the calculator, or scan the QR code on its screen with your phone.

    One account can have up to 3 calculators.

  5. 5

    Your first AI Solve

    Press MODE 4 to open Apps, then 1 for AI Solve. The screen becomes a live camera viewfinder. Point the camera on the back at a problem and press = to take the photo.

    MODE then 4 then 1 point, then =

    The answer and every step appear on the calculator. If the handwriting is unclear, it says so instead of guessing.

  6. 6

    The free month and the AI tutor plan

    Your first month of the AI tutor plan is free. It starts when you first link the calculator, and no card is needed.

    When the free month ends, the calculator tells you plainly. To keep using AI Solve, subscribe from your account page: $10 a month, or $100 a year. Cancel anytime. Without the plan, it’s still a full scientific calculator that works offline.

If something goes wrong

  • It won’t join Wi-Fi. Most often the network is 5 GHz only, or it has a login page. Wi-Fi help walks through the fixes.
  • The code isn’t accepted. Check all 8 characters and that you’re signed in, then show a new code on the calculator and try again.
  • AI Solve won’t start. Check that exam mode isn’t on. It turns off the AI, the camera and Wi-Fi until it ends after 12 hours or a teacher unlocks it with a USB cable.
  • Something else. See the FAQ or email hello@proofline.example.
