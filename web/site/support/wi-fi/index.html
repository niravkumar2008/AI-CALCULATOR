Wi-Fi help.

The calculator needs Wi-Fi only for AI Solve. Here’s how to get it online, and what to try when it won’t connect.

The short version: use 2.4 GHz home Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot. School networks with a login page aren’t supported. Everything except AI Solve works offline.

It needs 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi

The calculator connects to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only. Many home routers send out both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz, sometimes under the same name, and that usually works. A network that is 5 GHz only won’t show up or won’t connect.

How to tell:

  • A network with “5G” or “5GHz” at the end of its name is usually the 5 GHz one. Look for a twin without it.
  • The router’s label or its settings page often lists the bands it uses.

The quick fix: turn on your phone’s hotspot and connect the calculator to that. If your phone’s hotspot settings have an option for better compatibility or 2.4 GHz, turn it on.

School Wi-Fi with a login page

Some networks, often at schools, libraries and cafés, open a web page where you sign in or accept terms before you get online. The calculator can’t use those pages, so these networks aren’t supported.

Use a phone hotspot or home Wi-Fi instead. And remember the calculator works without Wi-Fi for everything except AI Solve.

Wrong password

  • Wi-Fi passwords are case-sensitive. Check capitals, and letters that look alike, such as O and 0 or l and 1.
  • Make sure you picked the right network. Neighbors’ networks can have similar names.
  • If typing on the keys is fiddly, try Set up with phone in the Wi-Fi menu instead.

Network not found

  • Check it’s a 2.4 GHz network (see above).
  • Move closer to the router, then look again.
  • If the network is hidden, the calculator may not list it. Try a phone hotspot.

Exam mode turns Wi-Fi off

That’s expected. Exam mode turns off the AI, the camera and Wi-Fi, and it shows a black status bar with a padlock, EXAM and a timer. Wi-Fi comes back when exam mode ends: by itself after 12 hours, or when a teacher unlocks it with a USB cable.

No Wi-Fi? It still works

AI Solve needs Wi-Fi. Everything else doesn’t: fractions, trig, logs, memory, replay and the rest of the calculator work offline, anywhere.

Menu names on this page follow the prototype and may change on the final firmware. Still stuck? Email hello@proofline.example, and never include your Wi-Fi password.
