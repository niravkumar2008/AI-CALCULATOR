Try the calculator.

This is our real calculator engine, running in your browser. Do a sum, then try AI Solve.

Open the apps.

MODE then 4

This runs the calculator’s own software. AI Solve and Type it use demo answers here, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Things to try

  • Do a sum with a fraction, then a trig function or a log. It works like a normal scientific calculator.
  • Press MODE 2 for statistics. Pick 1 (1-VAR), type a few numbers with = after each, then press AC and SHIFT 1 to find the mean and more. MODE 1 goes back.
  • Solve an equation: type X²=2, press SHIFT CALC, enter a guess and press =. To type X, press ALPHA then ); for the equals sign, ALPHA then CALC.
  • Solve a quadratic: press MODE 5 for equations, then 3 for aX²+bX+c=0. Type 1 = 2 = 5 = for x²+2x+5=0, then press = to solve. X1 is −1+2i; keep pressing = for X2 and the minimum point. MODE 1 goes back.
  • In the apps, open AI Solve and press = to “take” the demo photo.
  • Open Type it, type 3x+7=22 and send it.
  • Take the capitals quiz in Geography, or look up an element in the Periodic Table.
  • Use ◀ ▶ to move between apps and AC to go back.
