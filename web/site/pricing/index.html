Questions

About paying.

More answers in the FAQ.

What happens after the free month?

The calculator tells you plainly that the free month is over. To keep using AI Solve, subscribe from your account page: $10 a month, or $100 a year. There is no free tier after the first month.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime from your account page. No phone call, no form. The calculator keeps working as a full scientific calculator.

Who can buy it?

You must be 18 or older to buy it and hold the account. It’s made for students 13 and up.

When can I buy it?

Not yet. Our first prototype boards are ordered. We’re building 5 samples first, then moving to production; preorders are planned for 2027. We’re not taking payment yet. Orders will ship to US addresses only.

What about refunds and the warranty?

Those terms aren’t decided yet. We’ll publish them on the refunds and warranty page before orders open.
