Every calculator mode

Statistics, equations, matrices and more, built in.

The modes and keys a math or science class expects. The MODE menu has nine: COMP, STAT, TABLE, Apps, EQN, CMPLX, BASE-N, MATRIX and VECTOR. The calculator modes run offline, and they stay on in exam mode.

Statistics MODE 2

One-variable and two-variable statistics, with 7 regression types from linear to inverse. Up to 80 rows of data (fewer for paired data or with a frequency column). Sums, means, standard deviations, regression coefficients and estimates.

Function tables MODE 3

Type f(x), pick a start, end and step, and scroll through a table of x and f(x), up to 30 rows.

Equations MODE 5

Systems of 2 or 3 linear equations, and quadratic and cubic equations, with complex roots shown as a+bi. For a quadratic, it also gives the minimum or maximum point. A system with no answer or endless answers says “No Solution” or “Infinite Solution”.

Complex numbers MODE 6

Add, subtract, multiply and divide complex numbers, typed as a+bi or in polar form, r∠θ. Answers in either form, plus the argument, the conjugate and the absolute value.

Decimal, hex, binary, octal MODE 7

Whole-number math in DEC, HEX, BIN and OCT, with one key to switch the answer to another base. Logic operations too: and, or, xor, xnor, Not and Neg.

Matrices and vectors MODE 8 · 9

Matrices up to 3×3: add, subtract, multiply, determinant, transpose and inverse, kept as exact fractions. Vectors in 2D or 3D: dot product, cross product and magnitude.

CALC and SOLVE CALC

CALC asks for a value for each variable in an expression and works it out. SHIFT CALC solves an equation numerically from a starting guess, and says “Can’t Solve” when it finds no answer.

Integrals and derivatives ∫dx

Definite integrals with ∫dx, and the derivative at a point with SHIFT ∫dx (d/dx).

In your hand

Built like a real calculator.

Our own circuit board inside a familiar scientific-calculator shell.

  • Standard US key layout

    The keys are where you expect them, so there’s nothing new to learn.

  • A real rubber keymat

    The keys press like a calculator’s, not like a touchscreen.

  • Magnetic charging

    The charging plug snaps on with a magnet. The battery is rechargeable, 150 mAh.

  • No LED

    Nothing blinks or glows on the desk.

  • A sharp color display

    The calculator screens keep a classic LCD look. The apps, AI Solve’s answers and steps, and the camera viewfinder show in color.
