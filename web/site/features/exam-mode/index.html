Exam mode anyone can see.

One key sequence turns the AI, the camera, Wi-Fi and the apps off. The status bar turns black, so a teacher can tell at a glance.

Turn it on

Press SHIFT MODE 8 =.

SHIFT MODE 8 then =

What turns off

  • The AI. AI Solve is off.
  • The camera.
  • Wi-Fi.
  • Every app. Notes and saved answers can’t be opened until exam mode ends. About the apps

What still works

  • The full scientific calculator: fractions, trig, logs, memory and replay.
  • Statistics and tables. MODE 2 STAT, with regression, and MODE 3 TABLE.
  • Equations and number modes. MODE 5 EQN, MODE 6 CMPLX for complex numbers, MODE 7 BASE-N, MODE 8 MATRIX and MODE 9 VECTOR.
  • Solving and calculus. CALC, SOLVE, definite integrals (∫dx) and derivatives (d/dx). About the calculator
See it

A black status bar, a padlock and a timer.

In exam mode the status bar turns black and shows a padlock, the word EXAM and a timer that counts up from when exam mode started.

How it ends

  • It ends by itself after 12 hours.
  • Or a teacher unlocks it with a USB cable.

Tests and exams

Where you can use it, and where you can’t.

Not allowed on the SAT, ACT or AP exams, even in exam mode. It has Wi-Fi and a camera. Bring a standard calculator to those.

For a school test, ask your teacher first. Then turn on exam mode, or leave it at home.
