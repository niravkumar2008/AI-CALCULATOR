A calculator first. Apps when you want them.

It starts as a normal scientific calculator. Press MODE 4 and Apps opens in color: seven apps, from AI Solve to a full periodic table. Most of them work without Wi-Fi.

Try the apps in your browser
Two looks

Classic calculator. Then color.

Switch it on and it looks and works like a classic scientific calculator: a grey-green screen, dark digits, and the keys where you expect them.

Press MODE, then 4 for Apps. The screen turns to full color, one app at a time. Move with ◀ ▶, press = to open an app (or its number, 1 to 7), and AC to go back to the calculator, with your sum still there.

MODE then 4

App 1 of 7 · AI Solve

Snap a problem. See every step.

Needs Wi-Fi AI tutor plan

The screen becomes a live camera viewfinder. Point the camera on the back at a problem, press = to take the photo, and the answer appears on the calculator with every step worked out.

It works for math and science, plus basic finance. If the handwriting is unclear, it says so instead of guessing.

AI can make mistakes. That’s why it shows every step, so you can check the working.

More about AI Solve

App 2 of 7 · Type it

No photo? Type the problem.

Needs Wi-Fi AI tutor plan

An on-screen keyboard with letters, digits and math symbols. Move with the arrow keys and press = to type a character. The number and operator keys type straight in. Up to 160 characters.

Send it, and the answer comes back just like a photo answer, with every step.

App 3 of 7 · History

Your last 20 answers.

Works offline No plan needed

Every AI Solve and Type it answer is saved on the calculator itself, newest first. Open one to see the full answer and its steps again. Reading them doesn’t need Wi-Fi.

Clear the list whenever you like. It’s also cleared when the calculator is removed from the account.

App 4 of 7 · Notes

Ten short notes, always with you.

Works offline No plan needed

Keep up to 10 notes of up to 160 characters each, typed with the same on-screen keyboard. Open a note to read it, edit it or delete it.

Notes are saved on the calculator, so they’re still there after it’s switched off. In exam mode they can’t be opened.

App 5 of 7 · Geography

The world map, in your pocket.

Works offline No plan needed

A world map with 177 countries. Move from country to country with the arrow keys, zoom in, and see each capital marked on the map.

Open a country card (capital, continent, country code, latitude and longitude), browse an A–Z list, find a country by its first letters, show one continent at a time, or take the capitals quiz.

App 6 of 7 · Periodic Table

All 118 elements.

Works offline No plan needed

A full periodic table, every element colored by category, like the periodic table apps on graphing calculators.

Open any element for its details: mass, group, period, block, state at room temperature, electron configuration, electronegativity, density and more. List the elements by number, name or symbol, find one by its symbol or atomic number, highlight one category at a time, and graph trends across the table, such as electronegativity, ionization energy, atomic radius, density, melting and boiling points.

App 7 of 7 · Settings

Everything in one place.

Works offline No plan needed

Brightness, the screen sleep timer, Wi-Fi, the link code for your account, the screen look, exam mode, the battery level and the software version.

Setting up Wi-Fi and linking the calculator need Wi-Fi, of course. The rest works anywhere.

Exam mode

In exam mode, every app is off.

Press SHIFT MODE 8 =. The AI, the camera and Wi-Fi turn off, and so do all the apps. MODE 4 just says “Apps are off”.

Notes and History can’t be opened until exam mode ends, and turning exam mode on closes any app that’s open. The calculator keeps working, and the black status bar shows a teacher it’s on.
