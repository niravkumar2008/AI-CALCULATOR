How it works

Point. Press =. Learn.

  1. 1

    Point

    Press MODE 4 to open Apps, then 1 for AI Solve. The screen turns into a live camera viewfinder. Aim it at the problem.

  2. 2

    Press =

    Press = to take the photo. The calculator works out the answer in a few seconds.

  3. 3

    Learn the steps

    The answer appears on the calculator with every step. If the handwriting is unclear, it says so instead of guessing. AI can make mistakes. That’s why it shows every step, so you can check the working.

The details

Built to teach, not just to answer.

  • A live viewfinder

    Open AI Solve (MODE 4, then 1) and the screen shows what the camera on the back sees, so you can line up the problem.

  • Every step, not just the answer

    The answer appears on the calculator with the working, step by step, so you can follow it and do the next one yourself.

  • Honest when it can’t read it

    If the handwriting is unclear, it says so instead of guessing. Take another photo with the problem a little clearer.

  • Math, science and basic finance

    It works for STEM problems, math and science, and for basic finance.

The AI tutor plan

What AI Solve needs.

AI Solve comes with the AI tutor plan: $10 a month or $100 a year, with the first month free from when you first link the calculator.

  • Up to 300 solves a month. A fair-use limit. That’s about 10 a day.
  • Photos are not stored. Each photo is used only to solve that problem. We keep only usage counts.
  • Needs Wi-Fi. 2.4 GHz home Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot. Everything else on the calculator works offline.
  • Off in exam mode. Exam mode turns the AI, the camera and Wi-Fi off.
