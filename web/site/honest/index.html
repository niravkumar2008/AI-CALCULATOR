A tutor, not a shortcut.

It’s built to help students learn. Every answer shows the working, and exam mode switches the AI off where everyone can see it.

Three promises

What we built it to do, and not do.

  • Shows the steps

    Every answer comes with the working, so a student can follow it and do the next one alone.

    AI Solve doesn’t stop at a number. The steps appear on the calculator, right under the answer. And if the handwriting in a photo is unclear, it says so instead of guessing.

    AI can make mistakes. That’s why it shows every step, so you can check the working.

  • Not for the SAT, ACT or AP

    It has Wi-Fi and a camera, so it isn’t allowed on those exams, even in exam mode. Use a standard calculator there.

    For a school test, ask the teacher. Turn on exam mode, or leave it at home.

  • Clear to teachers, private for you

    Exam mode is something a teacher can see from across the room.

    Photos are used only to solve that problem and aren’t stored.

Exam mode

Off, where everyone can see it.

Four keys turn off the AI, the camera and Wi-Fi. The status bar turns black, with a padlock, EXAM and a timer, so a teacher can tell at a glance.

SHIFT MODE 8 =

Press SHIFT MODE 8 =.

  • The AI, camera and Wi-Fi turn off

    The calculator keeps working for normal math.

  • It ends by itself after 12 hours

    Or a teacher unlocks it with a USB cable.

  • Nothing glows

    There’s no LED. The black status bar is the signal.
