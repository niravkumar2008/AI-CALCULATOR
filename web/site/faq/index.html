Exams and school

Can I use it on the SAT, ACT or AP exams?

No. It has Wi-Fi and a camera, so it isn’t allowed on the SAT, ACT or AP exams, even in exam mode. Bring a standard calculator to those.

Can I use it on a school test?

Ask your teacher first. If they allow it, turn on exam mode before the test. If they don’t, leave it at home and use a standard calculator.

What does exam mode do?

Press SHIFT MODE 8 =. It turns off the AI, the camera and Wi-Fi, and turns the status bar black with a padlock, the word EXAM and a timer.

All the apps turn off too, so notes and saved answers can’t be opened. The calculator itself keeps working. Normal math doesn’t need Wi-Fi.

How does exam mode end?

It ends by itself after 12 hours, or a teacher unlocks it with a USB cable.

How can a teacher tell it’s in exam mode?

The status bar at the top of the screen is black and shows a padlock, EXAM and a timer, so it’s easy to see from across the room. There’s no LED, so nothing glows or blinks either way.

There’s more on the page for teachers.

Buying and the AI tutor plan

How much does it cost?

The calculator is $225. The AI tutor plan is $10 a month, or $100 a year (2 months free). It covers AI Solve (up to 300 solves a month, fair use) and software updates.

The first month is free. Your first year comes to $325 if you pay yearly ($225 + $100), or $335 if you pay monthly ($225 + 11 months at $10).

How does the free month work?

It starts when you first link the calculator to your account. No card is needed. When it ends, the calculator tells you plainly.

What happens after the free month?

The calculator tells you plainly that the free month is over. To keep using AI Solve, subscribe from your account page: $10 a month, or $100 a year. There is no free tier after the first month.

Does it work without the AI tutor plan?

Yes. Without the plan it’s still a full scientific calculator that works offline: fractions, trig, logs, memory, replay and more. The Geography, Periodic Table and Notes apps work without the plan too. AI Solve and Type it need the plan.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime from your account page. No phone call, no form.

Who can buy it?

You must be 18 or older to buy it and hold the account. It’s made for high school and college students, 13 and up.

Can one account have more than one calculator?

Yes, up to 3 calculators per account. They share one AI tutor plan and its 300 solves a month.

Setup and Wi-Fi

How do I set it up?

Charge it, then join Wi-Fi by typing on the keys or with your phone. The first time it joins Wi-Fi it registers itself. To use AI Solve, you then link it to your account once: on the calculator, press MODE then 4 to open Apps, then choose Settings and Link calculator to show the code, and enter it at the link page.

The setup guide walks through each step.

What Wi-Fi does it need?

2.4 GHz home Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot. There are no codes or web addresses to type to get it online. Linking it to your account for AI Solve uses a short code, once.

Will it work on school Wi-Fi?

Not if the network has a login page. Those aren’t supported. Use a phone hotspot or home Wi-Fi instead.

See Wi-Fi help for more fixes.

Why do I need to link the calculator?

Linking connects the calculator to your account, which is what AI Solve and the plan run on. You do it once: the calculator shows an 8-character code and a QR code, and you enter the code at the link page on any phone or computer.

Does it work without Wi-Fi?

Yes. It’s a full scientific calculator that works offline, and most of the apps do too. Only AI Solve and Type it need Wi-Fi.

Will it get updates?

Software updates come only with the AI tutor plan. Automatic updates over Wi-Fi are planned. Without the plan, the calculator still works fully offline; it just doesn’t get updates.

Using AI Solve

How does AI Solve work?

Press MODE 4 to open Apps, then 1 for AI Solve. The screen becomes a live camera viewfinder. Point it at the problem and press = to take the photo. The answer and every step appear on the calculator.

Which subjects does it work for?

Math and science, such as algebra, geometry, trigonometry, precalculus, calculus, physics and chemistry, plus basic finance.

What if it can’t read the problem?

If the handwriting is unclear, it says so instead of guessing. Take the photo again with the problem filling the viewfinder.

Does it just give the answer?

No. Every answer comes with the working, so a student can follow it and do the next one alone.

AI can make mistakes. That’s why it shows every step, so you can check the working.

How many problems can I solve?

The AI tutor plan includes up to 300 solves a month, under fair use. That’s about 10 a day. One plan covers the whole account, so up to 3 calculators share those 300 solves.

Apps

What apps does it have?

Press MODE 4 to open Apps. There are seven: AI Solve, Type it, History (your last 20 answers), Notes (up to 10 short notes), Geography (a world map with 177 countries, capitals and a quiz), Periodic Table (all 118 elements) and Settings.

See them all on the Apps page.

Do the apps work without Wi-Fi?

Most of them. Geography, Periodic Table, Notes and History work without Wi-Fi and without the AI tutor plan. AI Solve and Type it need Wi-Fi and the plan (or the free month).

Can students use notes in an exam?

No. Exam mode turns all the apps off. Notes and History can’t be opened until exam mode ends, and turning exam mode on closes any app that’s open.

Privacy and safety

What happens to the photos?

Each photo is used only to solve that problem. Photos are not stored. We keep only usage counts, such as how many solves you used this month.

Read the privacy policy for the details.

How do I remove a calculator from my account?

From your account page, any time. Once removed, it needs to be linked again before it can use AI Solve.

Hardware and battery

How long does the battery last?

We’ll measure it on our first samples and publish the real number. It has a 150 mAh rechargeable battery.

How does it charge?

With a magnetic charging connector that snaps on.

Where is the camera?

On the back. It’s a 5 MP autofocus camera, and it’s only used by AI Solve. Exam mode turns it off.

What kind of screen does it have?

A sharp color display.

Does it feel like a real calculator?

Yes. It’s our own circuit board in a familiar scientific-calculator shell, with the standard US key layout and a real rubber keymat. The full list is on the specs page.

Does it do statistics, equations, complex numbers and matrices?

Yes. MODE 2 is statistics: one-variable and two-variable data, 7 regression types, sums, means and standard deviations. MODE 3 makes a table of f(x). MODE 5 EQN solves systems of 2 or 3 linear equations and quadratic and cubic equations, complex roots included, and gives a quadratic’s minimum or maximum. MODE 6 CMPLX works with complex numbers, MODE 7 BASE-N with decimal, hex, binary and octal, MODE 8 MATRIX with matrices up to 3×3, and MODE 9 VECTOR with 2D and 3D vectors. CALC puts values into an expression, SHIFT CALC solves an equation numerically, and it does definite integrals and derivatives at a point.

All of it works offline, with or without the AI tutor plan, and it stays on in exam mode. More on the calculator page.
