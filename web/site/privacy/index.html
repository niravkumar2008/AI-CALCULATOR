Privacy policy

Template, last edited 10 October 2026

NEEDS LAWYER REVIEW. This is a draft template, not legal advice and not yet in force. Highlighted items are still to be decided.

The short version

  • Photos you take with AI Solve are sent to Claude, made by Anthropic, to solve the problem. We don’t store the photos.
  • We keep usage counts (for example, how many solves an account used this month) so we can apply the fair-use limit and bill correctly.
  • The calculator never stores an API key.
  • We don’t sell your data or show ads.

Who we are

Proofline is run by Proofline Corporation, address. Contact us at hello@proofline.example.

What we collect

  • Waitlist: your email address, which form you used, that you confirmed you’re 13 or older, and, if you came from a tagged link, the link’s campaign tags or referral code (for example “tiktok”). We use the email only to tell you when preorders open, and the tags only to count which posts bring people.
  • Account (later): the account holder’s email address and the calculators linked to the account.
  • Calculator: a device ID, the firmware version and usage counts. These let the calculator register itself and let us apply the 300-solves-a-month limit.
  • Payments (later): handled by our payment processor. Card details never reach our servers.

Photos and AI Solve

When you press = in AI Solve, the photo goes through our server to Anthropic’s Claude, which sends back the answer and the steps. We don’t keep the photo after it has been solved. Anthropic processes the photo for us as a subprocessor. Confirm Anthropic’s data-retention terms for our API plan and link them here.

Who processes data for us

  • Anthropic: solves the problems in photos (Claude).
  • Cloudflare: hosts this site, our server and the database.
  • Stripe: payments and subscriptions, once we start taking them.
  • Resend: sends sign-in and account emails.

Ages

Students using the calculator should be 13 or older. The account holder must be 18 or older. We don’t knowingly collect data from children under 13. Lawyer: COPPA and Anthropic’s minors policy.

How long we keep data

Waitlist emails: until preorders open plus N months, or until you ask us to delete yours. Account data: while the account is open. Usage counts: retention period.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and its data from the account page.
  • Leave the waitlist by emailing us, or with the link in any email we send.
  • Ask for a copy of your data by emailing us. State privacy rights (for example CCPA) to be confirmed.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date above and email account holders about anything important.
