Before you buy

What you need

The calculator works on its own. AI Solve needs two more things.

  • Wi-Fi, 2.4 GHz, or a phone hotspot

    Home Wi-Fi or a hotspot from your phone. School Wi-Fi with a login page isn’t supported. Wi-Fi help

  • An account

    You must be 18 or older to buy and hold the account. Link the calculator once with an 8-character code. Students should be 13 or older.
