AI tutor plan

The plan that turns on AI Solve. Snap a problem, press =, and learn every step.

Pick how you pay. Your free first month comes first, before either plan starts.
Join the waitlist Subscribe in your account
What’s included

Everything AI Solve does.

One plan for your account, paid monthly or yearly. There are no tiers and no add-ons.

  • AI Solve

    The screen becomes a camera viewfinder. Press = to take a photo of a math, science or basic finance problem.

  • Every step

    The answer and every step appear on the calculator. If the handwriting is unclear, it says so instead of guessing. AI can make mistakes. That’s why it shows every step, so you can check the working.

  • Type it

    No photo? Type the problem on the calculator’s on-screen keyboard instead, and get the same answer with every step. About Type it

  • Up to 300 solves a month

    AI Solve and Type it share them. One plan per account. Up to 3 calculators on the account share the 300 solves each month.

  • Software updates

    Updates for the calculator come with the plan. Automatic updates over Wi-Fi are planned.

First month free

It starts when you link the calculator.

No card needed for the free month. After that, there is no free tier.

  1. 1

    Link it once

    The calculator shows an 8-character code and a QR code. You enter it at the link page on any phone or computer.

  2. 2

    Use it free for a month

    AI Solve works right away. The calculator says plainly when the free month is over.

  3. 3

    Keep it for $10 a month or $100 a year

    Subscribe from your account to keep AI Solve. Or don’t: the calculator keeps working offline.

The plain terms

How it renews and how to cancel

Price
$10 a month, or $100 a year (2 months free), in US dollars.
Covers
One plan per account. Up to 3 calculators share 300 solves a month.
Free month
Your first month is free. It starts when you first link a calculator. No card needed. It comes first, before either plan starts.
Renewal
Once you subscribe, the plan renews automatically until you cancel: every month at $10, or every year at $100.
Cancel
Cancel online anytime from your account page.Refund terms for the subscription will be published before preorders open. Refunds and warranty
Billing
Billed separately from the calculator, through secure checkout.
Without the plan

Still a full calculator.

Fractions, trig, logs, memory, replay and more, offline. The Geography, Periodic Table, Notes and History apps work without the plan too. Only AI Solve and Type it need it.

Who holds the account

An adult, 18 or older.

The person who buys and holds the account must be 18 or older. Students should be 13 or older. One account can hold up to 3 calculators.
