How it works

Point. Press =. Learn.

  1. 1

    Point

    Press MODE 4 to open Apps, then 1 for AI Solve. The screen turns into a live camera viewfinder. Aim it at the problem.

  2. 2

    Press =

    Press = to take the photo. The calculator works out the answer in a few seconds.

  3. 3

    Learn the steps

    The answer appears on the calculator with every step. If the handwriting is unclear, it says so instead of guessing. AI can make mistakes. That’s why it shows every step, so you can check the working.

Built like a real calculator.

Our own circuit board inside a familiar scientific-calculator shell, with the standard US key layout.

  • Real keys

    A real rubber keymat, so the keys press like a calculator’s.

  • Apps in color

    The charging plug snaps on with a magnet.

    Nothing blinks or glows on the desk.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

Something else? Email hello@proofline.example.

Can I use it on the SAT, ACT or AP exams?

No. It has Wi-Fi and a camera, so it isn’t allowed on the SAT, ACT or AP exams, even in exam mode. Bring a standard calculator to those.

For a school test, ask your teacher: turn on exam mode, or leave it at home.

Who is it for?

High school and college students, 13 and up. You must be 18 or older to buy it and hold the account.

What happens to the photos?

Each photo is used only to solve that problem. Photos are not stored. We keep only usage counts, such as how many solves you used this month.

Read the privacy policy for the details.

What happens after the free month?

The calculator tells you plainly that the free month is over. To keep using AI Solve, subscribe from your account page: $10 a month, or $100 a year. There is no free tier after the first month.

When does it ship?

Not yet. Our first prototype boards are ordered. We’re building 5 samples first, then moving to production; preorders are planned for 2027. Join the waitlist to hear first.
