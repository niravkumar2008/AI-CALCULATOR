# Launch site copy deck

Voice: short, plain, confident, honest. No hype words ("revolutionary", "unlock", "seamless"),
no exclamation marks, sentence case. The AI Solve video sets the rhythm:
"Snap a problem." "Press = to shoot." "See every step." "Learn every step."

Facts come only from the brief, `web/site-src/PAGE_BRIEF.md` (it carries the 2026-10-11 decisions; the older
`docs/web/PLAN.md` D1 to D5 are partly out of date, e.g. D3's $15 price). If a line below isn't backed by a
fact, it isn't on the site.

## Key lines and alternatives

| Element | On the site | Alternatives | Why the chosen one |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero headline | Snap a problem. / Learn every step. | "Point. Press =. Learn." · "Every step, on a real calculator." | Matches the video's opening and end card, so the page and video feel like one thing. |
| Hero sub-line | An AI tutor inside a real scientific calculator. Point the camera, press =, and it shows the answer with every step worked out. | "A real scientific calculator with an AI tutor built in." | Says what it is, then the whole flow in one sentence. |
| Main CTA | Join the waitlist | "Get early access" · "Save my spot" | The brief's wording; honest that nothing is for sale yet. |
| Form label | Get an email when preorders open | "Your email" | Tells people exactly what they're signing up for. |
| Form note | No payment now. You must be 13 or older. Privacy policy | — | Answers "will I be charged?" and sets the age floor. |
| How it works | Point. Press =. Learn. | "Three steps to every answer." | Mirrors the three steps and the video. |
| Try it | Try the calculator. | "Press some keys." | Plain verb. |
| Honest by design (h2) | A tutor, not a shortcut. | "Honest by design." · "Built to teach." | Says the positioning in four words; "Honest by design" is kept as the eyebrow. |
| Exam mode | Press SHIFT MODE 8 =. The status bar turns black, so a teacher can tell at a glance. | — | From the firmware; no claims beyond it. It also turns all the apps behind MODE 4 off (said on the FAQ, apps and specs pages). |
| SAT/ACT/AP | It has Wi-Fi and a camera, so it isn't allowed on those exams, even in exam mode. | — | Required by plan section 9. Never "exam-approved". |
| Pricing (h2) | One price for the calculator. One for the tutor. | "$225, then $10 a month or $100 a year." | Explains the two parts before the numbers. |
| Pricing proof | Your first year: $225 + free month + 11 × $10 = $335 (or $225 + $100 = $325 yearly) | — | The clearest possible explanation; nothing hidden. |
| Closing | Be first in line. No payment now. | "Want one?" | Repeats the promise that matters most to the buyer. |

## Words we don't use

exam-approved, undetectable, stealth, hidden, panic, cheat, guaranteed, 100% accurate,
"for kids", any Casio name or model number, competitor names, reviews or ratings (we have none).
